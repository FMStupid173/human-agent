# Suno BGM Prompt

Use Instrumental mode. Target duration: 30 seconds.

## Style prompt

Warm layered electronic editorial soundtrack, 92 BPM, D minor, instrumental only, intimate and quietly confident. Begin with soft felt keys and a wide warm pad; gradually add interlocking glassy arpeggios, a restrained syncopated bass motif, rim percussion, subtle shakers, sparse counter-melody, and short elegant transition gestures. The arrangement should evolve every four to eight seconds without becoming busy, then clear the percussion for a full but calm final chord. Modern design-film aesthetic, reflective without becoming sad, leave generous space in the 1-4 kHz range for a slow Mandarin female voice-over, polished social media launch video mix.

## Timing and arrangement

- 0:00 immediate soft two-note motif, no long intro
- 0:03 add a restrained low pulse and a second key layer
- 0:09 short transition into the product reveal
- 0:12 establish interlocking arpeggios and light off-beat percussion
- 0:18 add syncopated bass and subtle counter-melody for the project section
- 0:21 introduce the fullest rhythmic layer for adversarial review
- 0:24 resolve into a clearer reliability section
- 0:27 remove dense percussion, open the pad, then land on a clean final chord at 0:30

## Avoid

No vocals, no spoken words, no cinematic trailer boom, no EDM drop, no aggressive kick, no busy hi-hats, no long riser, no cheerful corporate ukulele, no lo-fi vinyl noise, no sentimental piano ballad, no dense lead melody competing with narration.
